PROJECT OVERVIEW

Ravenous Sky is a third-person adventure across floating islands caught inside a supernatural hurricane. I led production for our 25-person game team, collaborating with design, art, programming and QA to turn an ambitious world into a coherent player experience. The level art and process imagery below represent work produced collaboratively by the team.

My role
Project Lead · Design Collaborator
Team / duration
25 people · 9 months
Unreal Engine 5PhotoshopIllustratorPerforce
APPROACH & PROCESS
Visual research

The team explored floating fragments, storm-darkened ruins and a contrasting oasis to establish a readable world and distinct environmental moods.

Traversal & blockouts

Levels were iterated around gliding, bracing against wind, stamina recovery and a sequence of recognizable landmarks.

Testing & refinement

I organized player testing and coordinated cross-team improvements to tutorial clarity, environmental guidance and overall progression.

Production delivery

I brought art, design and engineering work together through reviews, milestones and documentation, keeping scope aligned as the game evolved.

WORLD & LEVEL PROGRESSION

Three moods. One journey.

01 / THE STORM
Navigation in chaos

Fragmented islands, rain, heavy clouds and strong winds create an environment where beacon placement and recognizable silhouettes are essential to player orientation.

02 / THE EYE
A moment of calm

Warm architectural details and a sheltered sanctuary contrast with the hostile storm, giving the journey a deliberate change of pace.

03 / THE ESCAPE
Momentum & urgency

As the storm collapses, traversal shifts into a more urgent sequence; playtest findings helped the team reduce repetition and refine its rhythm.

LilyPad

A friendly space for people who make things.

↑
01 / 05

PROJECT OVERVIEW

LilyPad began with a specific question: how might a social art app make creativity feel welcoming rather than intimidating? Our five-person team designed a place where students and emerging artists could share doodles, find collaborators and participate in low-pressure prompts.

My role
UX/UI Designer · Web Developer
Team / duration
5 people
FigmaUX/UIWeb design
Project website ↗Figma design ↗
APPROACH & PROCESS
Discovery & audience

Surveyed and interviewed young creatives to understand participation barriers, habits and the desire for a supportive art community.

Structure & wireframes

Used personas and early layouts to organize the main journeys: discovering artwork, making a post, joining a challenge and connecting with peers.

Interface & identity

Developed inviting screens for drawing, social interaction, sharing and rewards, balancing playful visuals with clear navigation.

Presentation

Connected research, interface decisions and the final concept through a project website and Figma design files.

DESIGN CASE STUDY

The thinking behind the work

Design objective

Make discovery and social interaction feel easy for novice creators, without turning every activity into a competition.

Key visual choices

A soft, approachable art direction; recognizable actions; distinct screens for challenges, messages and creative posting.

Deliverables

Figma mockups and wireframes, mobile app screen designs, UX research and a project website.

Created in only 48 hours, To Boldly Escargot follows a tiny space snail traversing a Rubik’s Cube-shaped planet. I was responsible for VFX and UI art, helping the team communicate an unusual movement system with charm and clarity. The game won first place at the FIEA Game Jam.

My role
VFX Artist · UI Artist
Team / duration
4 people
UnityVFXUI design
Play on itch.io ↗Read FIEA article ↗
APPROACH & PROCESS
Quick visual language

Worked within a playful sci-fi tone so each effect and interface element felt part of Shellington’s strange little planet.

Readable interaction

Designed UI art and effects to help players track hazards, movement choices and changes in the cube environment.

48-hour production

Balanced essential visual feedback with polish, collaborating closely with the team during a tightly scoped jam.

Final presentation

Supported a cohesive playable build with recognizable win, loss and moment-to-moment feedback screens.

DESIGN CASE STUDY

The thinking behind the work

Design objective

Help players quickly understand movement across a cube with different biome rules.

Key visual choices

Playful effects, legible indicators and expressive success/failure feedback.

Outcome

1st Place, 2025 FIEA Game Jam; a playable four-person team project delivered in 48 hours.
